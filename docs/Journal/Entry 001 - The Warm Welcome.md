
**Date:** March 7, 2025
**Time:** 4:17 A.M. (Airport Terminal, Half-Conscious)

![[Pasted image 20250308103954.png]]

I touched down on the red-eye, my head full of _what-ifs_ and my mouth full of vending machine coffee. Can you believe it? Some bastard a few seats up took the last sugar packet. As if the cold coffee wasn’t despicable enough.

It had been six hours since my last donut—long enough for the memory to fade, but not the craving. I was left breathing in the fumes of a broken city and drinking the bitter tomes of this... whatever you can call it. This wasn’t coffee. This was a crime against every late-nighter with a shred of dignity left in their soul. *sip* ☕

The city greeted me like an old friend—one I hadn’t seen in longer than I cared to admit, and for good reason. It was gritty, cold, and reeked of deep-seated regret. But somewhere in that vapid darkness, I found it again—that familiar warmth. The comfort of an old haunt, the longing to reconnect. Sure, we'd changed, but our shared histories never _really_ left us.

Outside the terminal, I hailed a cab, but none came. Well—one did. But it was being chased by a squad of cop cars.  Classic... *sip* ☕

These guys always think they’re so clever, hopping in a cab like they’re the first idiot to try it. _“Get me outta here, FAST! Or you’re DEAD!”_ You just know the cabbie rolls their eyes. Just another Friday night for them.

So I walked. First motel I saw, first donut joint I passed. I needed food. That black curse of a drink had done me dirty,.. And the dozen donut holes I inhaled before the flight? Gone. Long gone.

That’s when I took a free ride down a flight of stairs.. *sip* ☕
![[Pasted image 20250308112819.png]]

Next thing I knew, I was being patched up by a medic with a **very** knowing look. The kind of look that said _"You again?"_. They had my number—sized me up in one glance. _This one needs glucose and a C-collar, for good measure._ A Penny-sized Happy Meal, delivered with efficiency.

At least my new acquaintances were kind enough to give me intel on the local donut dispensary. Small mercies.

It wasn’t a great start. But then again, I wasn’t here for a great start.  
I was here for a job.

This city needed me as much as I needed it.  
Or so I told myself. *sip* ☕
